Meet Dakshu

Not a cold robotic assistant. A patient, friendly companion who grows with you.

Talk like you're talking to a close friend.

Dakshu is built to break the barrier of sterile, impersonal chatbots. Whether you want to share a confusing math problem, practice an upcoming job interview, brainstorm a tech project, or just speak in casual Tanglish, Dakshu listens attentively, encourages your efforts, and never judges.

Warm & Encouraging Tone

Patient explanations, polite suggestions, and sincere positive motivation.
